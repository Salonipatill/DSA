Merge Sort is a Divide and Conquer sorting algorithm.

It works in three main steps:

Divide the array into two halves
Sort each half recutsively
Merge the two sorted halves

LOGIC:-

Step1:-
[8,3,5,2,9,1]

split the array into two halves.
[8,3,5]  [2,9,1]

Split again:
[8] [3,5]      [2] [9,1]

Split again:
[8] [3] [5] [2] [9] [1]

Now every part has only one element.

A single array is already sorted.


Step 2 - Merge the Arrays

Now we merge while sorting.

Merge 1
[3] and [5]
Result: [3, 5]

Merge 2
[8] and [3,5]
Compare step by step.

Result:
[3,5,8]


Merge 3
[9] and [1]

Result:

[1,9]

Merge 4
[2] and [1,9]

Result:

[1,2,9]


Final  Merge
[3,5,8]  and [1,2,9]

Compare step by step:

Final array:

1,2,3,5,8,9
